Humanity Flourishes

118. And yet we must remember that humanity flourishes not despite limitations, but often through them.

111. Every design choice reflects a vision of humanity.

Author's Context

I am frequently guilty of a technocratic mindset. Of a cold efficiency that sees me, and you, as a project to optimize. I don't like it, but it's true.

I’m a software developer. So it’s no surprise that Magnifica Humanitas stirred much inside me. Certainly hope, but also struggle.

How should I, with so little power or influence, respond to the call to “build a world where everyone can flourish”? If it’s true that “no one is so weak that they cannot play their part,” then what exactly is my part?

It took me a months to realize that I am not actually powerless.

There is a line from the encyclical that I want embossed or printed or somehow encapsulated forever, because it hit me like a brick: “Every design choice reflects a vision of humanity.”

If every choice at work reflects a vision of humanity, exactly what vision does my choice reflect?

So I distilled a short list of questions, directly from the encyclical, to guide my work. These questions immediately reveal the heart of what I'm building.

I use this list daily to question my choices and respond to the stirrings of my conscience. It has meant a great deal to me, and I wanted to share it, in the hopes others find it useful in making it actionable in their daily life.

- Edward
